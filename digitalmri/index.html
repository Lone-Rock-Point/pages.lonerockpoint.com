6 wks
Typical turnaround on a traditional modernization assessment. Sometimes longer. Often vague.
Faster than that, and more honest.

Most modernization conversations start the same way: a CIO asks how their site is doing, and the answer is slow, subjective, and buried in narrative. Digital MRI is built for the opposite.
