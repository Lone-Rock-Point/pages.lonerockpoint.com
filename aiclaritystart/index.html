01 / The Premise
Tools.
Last, not first.

Most businesses don't fail at AI because the tools are wrong. They fail because nobody figured out where AI moves the needle for the business before they started buying tools and chasing pilots.

We start with your business, not the platform.

A platform decision is a downstream decision. Made first, it locks in assumptions about your work that haven't been tested. Made later, it answers a question you can actually frame.

How it usually goes

Tools first, then problems.

A platform gets picked. The team retrofits problems to it. Six months in, the pilot quietly stalls.

How this engagement goes

Problems first, then tools.

We map where the leverage actually is. The tools come later, picked because the work demands them.

02 / How It Runs

Three phases. One blueprint.

Each phase builds on the last. From understanding how the business actually works, to surfacing the handful of opportunities that matter, to a sequenced plan you can act on.

01
Assess

How the business actually works.

Not the org chart version. The real version. Where time goes, what's repetitive, what frustrates you, where the team is stretched, what "good" looks like to you.

02
Identify

The handful that matter.

We surface the highest-impact opportunities for AI. Not 50 ideas. The handful that pay off, ranked by impact and feasibility.

03
Blueprint

A plan you can act on.

A written plan. What to do, in what order, with what tools, and what it'll take to get there. Built to be implemented, not shelved.

04 / Where This Is Headed

Most AI conversations stop at chat. Ours don't.

A lot of consultants get off the bus at Claude and ChatGPT. Useful tools. Not the destination. We design every Blueprint with the next step already in view.

"Where in the business is there a workflow that, with the right supervision, an agent could just run?"
Today

Chat-shaped AI

Claude. ChatGPT. Custom GPTs. Useful, and they're table stakes. The team types prompts and skims responses, faster than they used to but still in the loop on every keystroke.

Where this goes

Agentic AI

Agents doing real work autonomously. Your team supervising rather than driving. A different conversation, with different opportunities, and more durable ones.
